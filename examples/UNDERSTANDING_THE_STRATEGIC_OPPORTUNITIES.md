## 2. Design Strategy Breakdown

### Three Distinct Aesthetic Philosophies

#### Medallion Style
- **Concept**: Centralized Islamic geometric medallion
- **Visual Language**: Pure ornamental geometry, arabesque patterns
- **Cultural Context**: Manuscript illumination, sacred architecture
- **Target Appeal**: Traditionalists, customers seeking authentic Islamic art

#### Emblazon Style
- **Concept**: Architectural heraldic emblazonment
- **Visual Language**: Islamic mihrab arch, heraldic shield design
- **Cultural Context**: Mosque architecture, Islamic monuments
- **Target Appeal**: Architecture enthusiasts, heritage-conscious customers

#### Signature Style
- **Concept**: Mahal devotional sacred geometry
- **Visual Language**: Balanced architectural composition, spiritual geometry
- **Cultural Context**: Sufi sacred spaces, devotional art
- **Target Appeal**: Spiritual customers, modern traditionalists

### Strategic Style Selection

**Medallion**: Choose for **authenticity and tradition** - customers seeking pure Islamic geometric art

**Emblazon**: Choose for **architectural grandeur** - customers valuing Islamic monumental architecture

**Signature**: Choose for **spiritual elegance** - customers seeking subtle, contemplative beauty
